# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Pâtisserie Haloui is used by a small in-person administrative team in Tanger: the owner, branch operators, sales staff, and HR/admin staff. They work on Windows machines, stay on a local network or single PC, and need to move quickly between quote creation, invoicing, personnel paperwork, and daily branch revenue entry without cloud complexity.

## Product Purpose
Haloui System is a local-only operational tool for running the paperwork layer of a multi-branch pastry business. It centralizes commercial documents, staff records, attestations, branch revenue tracking, and multi-user traceability, with reliable persistence in SQLite and a print-first experience that produces customer-facing documents the business can hand over with confidence.

## Brand Personality
Dependable, respectful, efficient. The interface should feel calm and competent, like a well-organized office desk next to a receipt printer: warm enough to feel human, structured enough to reduce mistakes, and never flashy or playful at the expense of trust.

## Anti-references
Do not make this look like a generic startup SaaS dashboard, a dark-mode analytics template, a neon fintech admin, or a drab spreadsheet clone. Avoid decorative gradients, oversized KPI hero cards, novelty navigation, and any UI that looks more "tech demo" than daily business tool.

## Design Principles
1. Print parity first: on-screen data entry should map cleanly to the final printed document, because the printout is part of the product.
2. Familiar operations beat clever interactions: staff should recognize navigation, forms, statuses, and actions immediately.
3. Fast entry, low ambiguity: autocomplete, defaults, clear totals, and strong form hierarchy should minimize retyping and reduce accounting mistakes.
4. Traceability without friction: every important change should be attributable to a user, but auditing should not slow normal work.
5. Warm administrative clarity: the product should feel more considered and polished than a back-office tool, while staying restrained and operational.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility for the core workflow. Respect reduced-motion preferences, keep touch targets usable on smaller screens, use visible labels and focus states, and support French administrative content with bilingual Arabic/French print surfaces where required by the document templates.
